Concept

Still Here? began from a personal observation: I was spending 8–10 hours a day on screens, and I started to notice that what kept me scrolling was not always the content itself, but the anticipation of what might appear next.

The work turns that habit into a small behavioral loop. Repeated clicking produces fragmented images, empty states, rare rewards and interruptions, gradually shifting the experience from attraction toward awareness of one's own persistence.

States

Reward is deliberately uneven. Most clicks return incomplete images; sometimes the work withholds an image entirely, while rare and ultra-rare states briefly offer a more complete reward.
