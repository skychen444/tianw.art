Concept

Noise Portrait begins with the act of smoking.

Smoke becomes a stroke between the figure and reality, softening the boundary between presence and disappearance. Through thousands of algorithmic marks, the portrait is repeatedly interrupted but never fully lost.

What remains is a figure suspended between clarity and dissolution — a momentary distance from the noise within.
